# The Trojan Horse Attack

An interactive, two-chapter simulation that shows how one click on a fake job-interview email can let an attacker move through a company's internal servers, and what limiting access changes.

🔗 **Live app:** https://thetrojanhorse.lovable.app/

> Binance guarded the front door. The attacker used the other one.

## What it shows

- **Chapter 1, The attack:** a staff laptop receives a fake "Senior Engineer" interview invite. You follow what happens after the click as the attacker moves toward internal servers.
- **Chapter 2, The fix:** how limited access (least privilege) contains the damage.

## Honesty notes

- The attack path follows public reporting, except one step marked with a dashed line, which is my own assumption.
- The email, installer, screens and logs are illustrative.
- This is a simulation. Not affiliated with Binance or any vendor.

## Why I built it

To practice turning a complex security incident into a story a non-technical audience can follow in under two minutes, and to show where product and design decisions (access limits, onboarding flows) can reduce risk.
